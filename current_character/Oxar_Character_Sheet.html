FIGHTING TECHNIQUES

Standard Turn

  • Two greataxe swings (+7, 1d12+7 each with HWM). Pick a target whose neighbor you also want dead — a hit lets Cleave carry into the creature next to it (free 1d12, once/turn)
  • Savage Attacker on the biggest damage roll of the turn (1/turn)
  • Spend a superiority die when it matters: Goading (DC 15 WIS) on the enemy you want locked onto you

Opening Moves

  • Out of reach? Throw a handaxe (+7, 20/60) — a hit triggers Vex: Advantage on your next attack vs. that target
  • Need to close fast? Lunging Attack (Bonus Action, 1 die): Dash, come in ≥5 ft straight → +1d8 on a melee hit this turn

Nova Turn (the decisive round — boss revealed, ally down, breach to hold)

  1. Prime Earth's Grasp beforehand (1/long rest): your first hit forces DC 13 STR save → target Restrained — your remaining attacks and the whole party's get Advantage, its attacks have Disadvantage
  2. Attack (2 swings) → Action Surge → Attack again = 4 × (1d12+7)
  3. Maneuver die on key hits (max 1 per attack)
  4. Any kill or crit → Hew (Bonus Action): 5th swing

Your One Reaction — spend it wisely

  1. Rune Stone Shield (1/short rest) — a big attack is about to hit by ≤5? AC 16 → 21, likely turning it into a miss. Auto-negates Magic Missile
  2. Riposte (1 die) — melee enemy missed you? Punish: +7, 1d12+4+1d8
  3. Opportunity Attack — enemy leaves your reach

Staying Alive

  • Second Wind (Bonus Action, 3/rest): heal 1d10+5 and shift 15 ft with no Opportunity Attacks — heal-and-reposition, or escape a surround
  • Playing tank: Goad the biggest hitter — Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you, and you have AC 16 + the Shield stone
  • Heroic Inspiration: hold it to re-roll a failed save or a just-missed key attack (you re-roll after seeing the result)

Resource Pacing

  • Superiority dice (4): refresh on short rest — spend 1-2 per fight, don't hoard
  • Action Surge (1/rest): for the decisive round, not round one by habit
  • Rune Stone: Shield freely (short rest); Earth's Grasp only once per day — save it for the fight that matters
